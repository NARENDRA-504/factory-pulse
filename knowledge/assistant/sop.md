# SOP Operator
Status: Training guidance; site approval pending.
1. Confirm your assigned machine and shift before using the displayed readings.
2. Check timestamps, units and data quality. If readings are missing or stale, report the data issue rather than assuming normal operation.
3. Report abnormal readings or a safety concern through Report Problem and contact your supervisor. Follow the site's approved safety procedure; this app does not authorize repair or restart.

# SOP Supervisor
Status: Training guidance; site approval pending.
1. Review your assigned machines and operators, then prioritize open incidents by severity and current evidence.
2. Assign the concern to an authorized technician and record the handover. Confirm coverage for affected workstations.
3. Follow up on investigation and verification notes before accepting a resolved status. Escalate safety concerns through the site's approved process.

# SOP Maintenance
Status: Training guidance; site approval pending.
1. Open the assigned incident and compare recorded symptoms with timestamped machine readings.
2. Follow the equipment-specific, site-approved isolation and inspection procedure. These training notes do not supply isolation, repair or restart instructions.
3. Record findings and actions. Close the incident only after the required verification and authorization, with meaningful closure notes.

# SOP Manager
Status: Training guidance; site approval pending.
1. Review plant and line comparisons using the same time window and measurement definitions.
2. Check missing data, downtime coverage and configured cost assumptions before interpreting production or financial results.
3. Review major risks with the supervisor and maintenance team, and record decisions through the established factory process.

# SOP Admin
Status: Training guidance; site approval pending.
1. Review user access, machine assignments and recent activity before changing configuration.
2. Grant only the access required by the person's role. Check machine identifiers and assignments when onboarding equipment.
3. Verify data availability and audit records after a change. Keep secrets out of reports and chat, and use the approved password reset process.

# SOP production
1. Confirm the selected machine, shift and reporting window.
2. Compare good plus scrap counts with the configured target. Treat missing counters and reset boundaries explicitly.
3. Record production concerns through the incident workflow; do not infer attendance or actual working time from login duration.

# SOP machines
1. Check machine identity, reading timestamp, engineering unit and data-quality flag.
2. Compare readings with configured bounds. These bounds are not certified safety limits.
3. Report a concern with its machine and sensor context; follow the approved site response procedure.

# SOP incidents
1. Select an authorized machine and describe the observed concern, severity and relevant readings.
2. Check existing open incidents before creating another report for the same concern.
3. Track assignment, investigation and resolution notes. A status change alone is not evidence of a physical repair.

# SOP maintenance
1. Review assigned work, priority and incident history.
2. Follow the approved equipment inspection checklist and document findings and actions.
3. Record verification and required authorization before resolving a ticket; check that linked work orders reflect completion.

# SOP analysis
1. Confirm filters, period, units and the records included in the comparison.
2. Distinguish unavailable data, observed downtime, modeled production and configured cost assumptions.
3. Use findings to prioritize review; do not treat a chart or AI suggestion as equipment operating authorization.

# SOP access
1. Confirm the person's identity, role and assigned team before changing access.
2. Apply only authorized user or machine changes through the Admin controls.
3. Review the resulting assignment and activity log; never disclose existing passwords or credentials.
