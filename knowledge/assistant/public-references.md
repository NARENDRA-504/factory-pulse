# Public machine-safety references
Reference review: 2026-11-23. OSHA's hazardous-energy overview describes risks from unexpected startup and stored energy during servicing. Reference: https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy/ . This is US educational/regulatory material, not proof of local compliance. FactoryPulse must use the applicable site-approved equipment procedure through trained authorized personnel. Do not invent isolation points, switch sequences, reset commands or approval identities from general public guidance.

# Selecting the correct manufacturer manual
Haas's official directory is https://haascnc.com/owners/Service/operators-manual.html . The site owner must verify exact Haas model, control generation, fitted options and manual revision. A Haas manual must not be applied to Mazak or DMG MORI equipment. Public availability does not approve a local SOP. Training guidance, incident closure and a normal chart never authorize physical restart.

# Measured bearing research versus live model validation
Case Western Reserve University's Bearing Data Center publishes measured acceleration and motor speed from normal and deliberately faulted bearings on a controlled motor rig. Source: https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter/download-data-file . This is useful for a separate bearing-diagnostics study. It does not supply FactoryPulse's full seven-channel contract or prove future failures on our machines. Hold out complete recordings/experimental conditions; neighboring windows from one recording must not cross train and test. Do not substitute acceleration for velocity in mm/s, invent missing sensor channels, or claim site validation.

# Existing synthetic practice benchmark
UCI AI4I 2020 has 10,000 synthetic rows. Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601/ai4i+2020+predictive+maintenance+dataset . FactoryPulse's separate practice script uses it for current-row binary failure classification. It is not the deployed model or a future-warning diagnosis. Its results cannot approve equipment SOPs or turn on model alerts.
